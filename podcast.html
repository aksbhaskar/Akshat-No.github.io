About the Podcast

Decoded is a podcast that makes complex ideas in economics and technology accessible to curious minds, no jargon, no prerequisites. Each episode features conversations with economists, technologists, and policy thinkers breaking down the ideas shaping our world: from financial systems and AI to public policy and social innovation. The show is rooted in a simple conviction: that the most important ideas shouldn't be locked behind academic walls.
