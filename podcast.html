Liner Notes

Decoded makes complex ideas in economics and technology accessible to curious minds, no jargon, no prerequisites. Each episode is a conversation with economists, technologists, and policy thinkers breaking down the ideas shaping our world. The show is rooted in a simple conviction: the most important ideas shouldn't be locked behind academic walls.

Host Akshat Bhaskar, New Delhi
Production Independent. No sponsors, no backing, just ideas.
