Preamble

What follows is a record of Akshat's formal schooling, residential research programmes, and competitive fellowships, assembled between 2022 and the present. His foundation was a standard CBSE curriculum at one of India's most competitive schools; everything else was built around it. Admitted into the country's most selective mathematics programme at a 3.2% acceptance rate, appointed to a paid research apprenticeship in analytic number theory, and accepted into competitive programmes internationally, none of it accessed through IB, A-levels, or the infrastructure of educational consultants.

Entries are ordered roughly by academic weight, not chronology. Dates are of participation.
