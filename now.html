research

I am currently working on a study that tests whether fear of missing out (FOMO) and conscientiousness interact to predict financial misinformation sharing. It extends Professor Kakkar’s framework on political ideology, conscientiousness, and fake news sharing into a financial context, with FOMO taking the place of ideology. checkout more details here, and the GitHub here.

building

back to running the Edunomix India Chapter day to day, the 501(c)(3) chapter i founded to teach economics to students who need it. i'd stepped back for a while under other commitments (don't worry guys i aced my board exams), and it is good to be in it properly again.

right now, that mostly means reviewing entries for the second edition of our nationwide essay competition. the submission deadline has now passed, and I’m mostly spending my time reading and evaluating essays from students in Classes 6 to 12, as well as first-year university and gap-year students, responding to five prompts on questions that matter for India, along with our other judges from our wonderful panel. the top entries will go on to join the Edunomix India research team, and I am really excited to soon announce the winners :)

we have also partnered again with Learn and Teach UA for this season's Ukraine Speaks English seminars, free financial education for students across Ukraine whose schooling has been disrupted.
