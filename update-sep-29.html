Cary S. Krosinsky on Decoded

am delighted to share that our episodes with Cary S. Krosinsky are now live on Spotify!

Cary teaches at Yale, Brown, and NYU Stern, co-founded the Carbon Tracker Initiative, advises the New York State Common Retirement Fund, and has written eight books on sustainable finance, including Sustainable Investing and his latest, Transition Momentum. I was extremely delighted when he agreed to my request to sit down for a live conversation and student Q&A with our team and the students who joined us for the webinar.

We split the event into two parts (hence the two part episode series), our associate team teaching the fundamentals, then our full conversation with Cary. I would also like to thank our incredible associates Vidyanshi Mittal, Aly Muhammad Dadwani, and Akshara Durugkar for their AMAZING teaching!

I would love to hear your thoughts on these episodes, both of which are available on the streaming platform of your choice :)
