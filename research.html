Akshat is interested in the economics of information and governance: how institutions encode knowledge, how policy alters incentive structures, and where rigorous mathematical tools can illuminate questions that prose alone cannot. Current work spans AI policy, tail risk in financial markets, education policy, and algebraic number theory.
