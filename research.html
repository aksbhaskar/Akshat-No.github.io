Research Statement

Akshat is interested in the economics of information and governance: how institutions encode knowledge, how policy alters incentive structures, and where rigorous mathematical tools can illuminate questions that prose alone cannot. Current work spans AI policy, tail risk in financial markets, education policy, and algebraic number theory.

Ongoing 2026 [cs.CL]
Financial Misinformation on Indian Social Media: Multilingual Detection and the Traits That Predict Sharing Indian School of Business · Under faculty mentorship

Examining how financial misinformation spreads across Indian social media. The corpus is multilingual and code-mixed, so detection runs on MuRIL and XLM-RoBERTa rather than the usual English-only models. Paired with validated psychological instruments, the BFI-2-XS personality inventory and the Fear of Missing Out scale, to identify which individual traits predict sharing behaviour rather than only which posts are false.
