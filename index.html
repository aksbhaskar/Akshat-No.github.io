Abstract

Before even finishing high school, Akshat stood in Rashtrapati Bhavan, felicitated by the President of India for his work on Artificial Intelligence. He drafted policy at the Ministry of Education, completed university-level pure mathematics at the Lodha Genius Programme (3.2% acceptance), and conducted research under a University of Pennsylvania professor that became a peer-reviewed, internationally published paper, presented at a national conference where he was the only high schooler in the room. He founded his own NGO chapter in India before turning 16, earning international recognition in financial literacy. He has researched at the Indian School of Business and Finance, reached 48,000+ young Indians as CFO of TaxCity, and ranked in the top 0.06% of 67,000+ students statewide (Rank 44).

None of it was bought, coached, or counselled into existence. No educational counsellor, no paid programmes, no IB, no A-levels. Just a CBSE student with curiosity and the willingness to try.
