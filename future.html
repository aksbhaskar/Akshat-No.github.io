more on me

i'm interested in economics, anthropology, philosophy, and mathematics, and mostly in the questions that sit where they overlap.

i started early. while still in school i founded the india chapter of edunomix, a nonprofit that teaches economics to kids who never get offered it, and ran a nationwide essay competition across 19 states and 54 schools.

i ran finance as cfo of taxcity, a card game that teaches tax filing. it reached 79,000+ students across 27 countries and raised $120k+ in grants. we have since wound it down since even with the grants, giving away thousands of kits for free across india stopped being financially sustainable. we donated what was left to a few government schools we had visited, to help them build a better financial-literacy curriculum.

somewhere in there i started decoded, a podcast where i sit down with economists and technologists and make their ideas and discoveries make sense to the general public. it's won over three spotify for creators awards and passed 300 listening hours. i just recorded an episode with m. ram murty, go check it out.

i do research too. i wrote an ai-policy paper that got published and presented at a national conference where i was the only high schooler, and now i'm studying how financial misinformation spreads across indian social media at the indian school of business (isb).

before finishing high school i got felicitated by the president of india for my work on ai. i went to yc's first startup school in india, got into buildspace when i was in 10th, and built a bunch of other random stuff. some of it worked, most didn't, but i sure learned a lot.

right now i'm the founding technical and recruitment lead at docsdocs, an apple-backed startup, and i still run edunomix and decoded. i also like to read a lot.

sometimes i write things down. if any of this overlaps with what you're building, startup, podcast, research, non profit, whatever, ping me at bhaskarakshat22@gmail.com. i usually reply to short emails with a clear ask.
