Been off LinkedIn for a few days, out traveling and meeting family, so here's what happened while I was gone:

The second edition of the Edunomix Institute India Essay Competition is officially live, and submissions are open! It's a national essay competition for students in Classes 6 to 12, as well as first year university students and gap year students, with five prompts on questions that matter for India right now. The top essays are judged by economics scholars from Harvard University, University of California, Berkeley, Cornell University, and University of Warwick. Deadline is September 30. Link in the comments, pick a prompt and write something you'd want to defend :)

And if NYAS is new to you: I recently completed my Junior Academy Fellowship with them, working on an energy infrastructure project under Dr. Nandhini Sivakumar, Postdoctoral Research Scientist at the Motor Neuron Center, Columbia University. Apply if you're a student looking for real research mentorship, it's a great opportunity!

Welcome to the team, both of you!
