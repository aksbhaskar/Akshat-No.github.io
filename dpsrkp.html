§1 School Memoir

I joined Delhi Public School, R.K. Puram in grade 9th after an entrance examination. I took the subjects Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics, Economics, English in Grade 11th and 12th.

President Student Council, Class of '26

I was appointed the President of the Student Council, Class of '26 after rigorous application, interview, and recommendation rounds. The finalists had to go through a 7-round interview process. After being appointed, I was sworn as the President Student Council by Shri V. K. Shunglu, ninth Comptroller and Auditor General of India and Vice-Chairman of the DPS Society.
